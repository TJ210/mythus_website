The Mythos Unveiled

A Pantheon of
Flavors & Nightlife

Perched high above Hyderabad's vibrant skyline, Mythus Brewpub & Kitchen is a sanctuary where ancient Mediterranean legend merges with the electric pulse of modern nightlife. Our architecture is inspired by the grandeur of Greek mythology—featuring grand marble-texture finishes, soaring pillars, and warm, ambient lighting reminiscent of Mount Olympus under starlight.

From freshly crafted microbrews developed by master brewers to signature smoke-infused cocktails, each offering is a recipe of artistic storytelling. Whether you are gathered for conversations in our spacious alfresco dining area, celebrating with friends, or dancing to the beats of top DJs under neon nights, Mythus is designed as an Instagram-worthy escape from the ordinary.

Here, dining is not just a meal; it is an epic celebration. Join us on the rooftop, and write your own mythical night.
